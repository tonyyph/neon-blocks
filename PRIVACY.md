# Privacy Policy — Neon Blocks

_Last updated: 30 September 2026_

Neon Blocks is an offline game.

- **No account.** You never sign in or register.
- **No personal data is collected.** The app does not collect, store, send or share any
  personal information.
- **No tracking, analytics or advertising.** The app contains no analytics SDKs, advertising
  SDKs or trackers, and does not use the advertising identifier.
- **No internet connection is used.** The game runs entirely on your device.
- **What is stored on your device:** your settings (sound, haptics, ghost piece) and your
  game statistics (high score, games played, best lines, best level). They stay on your device,
  are never transmitted, and are removed when you uninstall the app. You can erase your
  statistics at any time with Settings → Reset High Score.
- **Permissions:** the app requests no permissions.
- **Children:** the app collects no data from anyone, including children.

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new date.
