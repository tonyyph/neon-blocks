# Privacy Policy — Neon Blocks

_Last updated: 2 October 2026_

Published at <https://neonblocks-app.vercel.app/privacy> (English and Vietnamese). Keep this file and
`site/privacy/index.html` in step.

Neon Blocks is an offline game.

- **No account.** You never sign in or register.
- **No personal data is collected.** The app does not collect, send or share any personal
  information.
- **No tracking, analytics or advertising.** The app contains no analytics, advertising or
  tracking SDKs and does not use the advertising identifier.
- **No internet connection is used.** The game runs entirely on your device.
- **What is stored on your device:** your settings (language, theme, sound, haptics, ghost piece),
  your records and statistics, your Daily challenge history and your awards. They stay on your
  device, are never transmitted, and are removed when you delete the app. You can erase your
  progress at any time with Settings → Reset progress.
- **Sharing:** when you tap Share after a game, the app makes an image of your result on your
  device and hands it to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes next is your choice; the app receives
  nothing back.
- **Permissions:** the app requests no permissions.
- **Children:** the app collects no data from anyone, including children.

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new date.
